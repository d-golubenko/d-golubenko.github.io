01 / Hello, world

About me

I am a membrane scientist and a postdoctoral fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, working with Prof. Yingchao Dong. I develop ion-exchange and ion-selective polymer membranes, from molecular design to laboratory-scale separation processes.

My research combines functional-polymer chemistry, membrane modification, and electrochemical characterization to understand and control ion transport. I am interested in improving the balance between conductivity, selectivity, and stability for water reuse, resource recovery, battery recycling, and energy conversion.

Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen in October 2025, I was a postdoctoral associate at the NYU Abu Dhabi Water Research Center (2023–2025), working with Prof. Nidal Hilal. I previously worked at the Kurnakov Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry, Russian Academy of Sciences.

I received my Ph.D. from the Kurnakov Institute in 2021, studying radiation-grafted ion-exchange membranes, and a Specialist Degree in Fundamental and Applied Chemistry from Lomonosov Moscow State University in 2017.

02 / Research interests

Research

  • Selective ion transport. Membrane chemistry, surface modification, organic-inorganic hybrid materials, and controlled hydration for separating ions of the same charge.
  • Resource recovery. Selective electrodialysis and diffusion dialysis for acid recovery, battery leachate processing, brine mining, and nutrient recovery.
  • Membranes for energy. Radiation- and UV-grafted ion-conducting polymers for reverse electrodialysis and proton-exchange-membrane fuel cells.
  • Transport mechanisms. Electrochemical measurements, structural characterization, and modelling to connect membrane structure with separation performance.
  • Molecular modelling. My current work also explores molecular-level mechanisms of ion transport in polymer membranes through computational modelling.
